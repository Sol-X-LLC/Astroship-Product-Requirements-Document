People keep asking why the grid is the size it is, so here is the honest answer: we played hundreds of games on every size we could think of, and 14 by 10 is the one where every match feels tense but never cramped. Big enough to hide a fleet, small enough that you are always one good guess away from finding it.

Fun Fact: There are more possible games of chess than there are atoms in the observable universe. We are not trying to beat that, we just want your next match to feel like it could go either way.

A little peek at what is coming: Astroship is going to support the Apple Pencil. Tap a square to fire, drag to sweep the grid, and place your fleet like you are drawing it on a real battle chart. It is the closest the game has ever felt to the pencil-and-paper original, and it is going to be a joy to play.

Astroship was "Built with Love" and you can sign up for the iOS TestFlight Beta for free today at www.astroship.store/beta

Fun Fact: Graphite was first used in pencils in the 1500s, after a storm in England uncovered a pure deposit of it. Five hundred years later we are still drawing with it — now it just has a battery.

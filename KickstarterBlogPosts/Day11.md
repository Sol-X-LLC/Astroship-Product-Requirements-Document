This one is for the tinkerers. Astroship runs on Apple's free Game Center servers, but if you would rather run your own, you can. The server code is open source, and you can host a game on your Mac or your Linux box at home with Tailscale. Your game, your server, your rules. That is a promise, not a feature list.

Astroship was "Built with Love" and you can sign up for the iOS TestFlight Beta for free today at www.astroship.store/beta

Fun Fact: The first email was sent in 1971, and the sender picked the @ symbol because it was the least-used key on the keyboard. Sometimes the best decisions are the boring ones.

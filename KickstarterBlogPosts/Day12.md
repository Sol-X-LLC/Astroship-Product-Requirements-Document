Hello World,

We built Astroship so you never have to rely on color alone to know what is happening. Every ship, every hit, every warning is a shape and an outline first, a color second. Same for sound — nothing important in this game is ever sound-only. If you can see the grid, you can play the game. Full stop.

Astroship was "Built with Love" and you can sign up for the iOS TestFlight Beta for free today at www.astroship.store/beta

Fun Fact: About 1 in 12 men and 1 in 200 women have some form of color vision deficiency. That is millions of players, and they deserve a game that was designed with them in mind from day one.
